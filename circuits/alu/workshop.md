Use this circuit as the calculating core of a computer or calculator, wherever values must be added, subtracted, shifted or combined with logic.

Give it two binary numbers, a and b, and a code from 0 to 7 for the operation: 0 adds, 1 subtracts, 2 is and, 3 is or, 4 is xor, 5 shifts a up one place, 6 shifts a down one place, 7 is not a. The flag gives the carry for add, the borrow for subtract, or the bit shifted out for shifts.
