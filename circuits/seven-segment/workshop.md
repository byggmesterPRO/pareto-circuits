Use this in a creation that shows one digit on a display, such as a counter or a calculator readout. It takes hexadecimal digits, so the display can also show A to F.

Connect the digit to the input gates, least significant bit first. Each output gate drives one segment, in the order a to g: a is the top bar, b the upper right, c the lower right, d the bottom, e the lower left, f the upper left and g the middle bar.
